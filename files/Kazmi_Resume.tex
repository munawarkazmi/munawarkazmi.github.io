\gap
\entry{LLM safety verifier and llm-nav-shield}{\href{https://github.com/munawarkazmi/llm-nav-shield}{github.com/munawarkazmi/llm-nav-shield}}
\begin{itemize}
\item A deterministic gate between LLM planners and Nav2 caught \textbf{35/35 and 32/32 unsafe proposals} from two models
with zero misses; a provably correct planner then recovered \textbf{38/38} to verified-safe paths and halted in
\textbf{10/10} cases where no safe path exists. Every number is replayed in CI.
\end{itemize}

passes 39 and the contract passes 32.
