\section{Research Projects}

\entry{legibility-bounds: certified limits on legible motion}{2026 \;\textperiodcentered\; \href{https://github.com/munawarkazmi/legibility-bounds}{github.com/munawarkazmi/legibility-bounds}}
\sub{Sole author. Python, computational geometry, exact predicates.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Proved a ceiling \textbf{no trajectory within the path budget can exceed}, where a search can only report the clarity
it happened to find. \textbf{32 of 32 certified intervals hold with zero violations}; the widest gap is 0.0567 and the
narrowest 0.0064.
\item The same argument prices safety: \textbf{3 of 8 keep-out pairs certify a positive cost}; the other 5 certify nothing and
are reported as nothing.
\item Stated limit: the observer model is exactly reproducible and has not been validated against people.
\end{itemize}
\gap
\entry{plan-failure-bench}{2026 \;\textperiodcentered\; \href{https://github.com/munawarkazmi/plan-failure-bench}{github.com/munawarkazmi/plan-failure-bench}}
\sub{Sole author. Python, PDDL, differential testing, CI.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Designed \textbf{60 proof-carrying instructions} over two symbolic environments; every ground-truth label is a
mechanical proof re-verified by \textbf{589 CI tests} on each commit.
\item \textbf{Judge-free scoring}: a deterministic checker, differentially tested against an independent PDDL toolchain,
assigns one verdict per response. Detection is never reported without its paired false positive count.
\item Ran an \textbf{18-run grid over four models}, plain and semantically obfuscated; versioning the obfuscation caught
and retired two artefacts of its own first token scheme.
\end{itemize}
\gap
\entry{legible-motion-bench (pilot)}{2026 \;\textperiodcentered\; \href{https://github.com/munawarkazmi/legible-motion-bench}{github.com/munawarkazmi/legible-motion-bench}}
\begin{itemize}
\item Measures legibility against path cost and constraint satisfaction, computed exactly, with no human rater and
no model judge. In a three-model pilot the models called \textbf{116 of 120} of their own trajectories legible,
including all 25 that were not physically possible. One temperature and three models: a pilot, not a finding.
\end{itemize}
\gap
\entry{Verbal and non-verbal intent communication on the JD humanoid}{2023 - 2025}
\sub{MSc thesis study, University of Hertfordshire. Isaac Sim digital twin, ROS2, physical deployment. Ethics approval cSPECS/CL/UH/05098.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Built a sim-to-real pipeline so that a gestural and a spoken condition could be delivered identically to
\textbf{20 participants}, within-subjects and counterbalanced, measured on the Robotic Social Attributes Scale.
\item Speech conveyed intent more clearly than gesture (4.9 against 5.8 on a 7-point scale, $p=0.017$, $d=0.58$), while
engagement and warmth were comparable. Every gestural failure was a failure of joint attention.
\end{itemize}
